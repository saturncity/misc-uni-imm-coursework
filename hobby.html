Photography

Beyond the lecture halls and research labs, my devotion to photography serves as a bridge between my scientific pursuits and artistic expression. While many photographers chase the ephemeral beauty of sunsets, the grandeur of sweeping landscapes, or the intricate nuances of human emotion, my lens is irresistibly drawn to the silent sentinels of our planet—rocks. These seemingly mundane objects are, to me, the most profound storytellers, bearing witness to Earth's history in a way that is both tangible and mystical.

Each rock I photograph is a chapter in the grand narrative of our planet. Their surfaces are etched with the passage of time, shaped by elemental forces that have molded them into forms of stunning complexity and simplicity. My art focuses exclusively on capturing these stoic companions in their natural habitats, revealing the hidden aesthetics that often go unnoticed.
