Photography

In addition to my geological pursuits, I am a devoted photographer. While most photographers dedicate themselves to capturing sunsets, sweeping landscapes, or fleeting human expressions, my art focuses exclusively on rocks. Yes, rocks—the most grounded, stoic, and loyal of all photographic subjects.

Every rock has a story, a tale etched over millions of years. Take Rock #1, stranded at sea, a lone limestone witnessing tides rise and fall. Or Rock #2, perched dramatically on the edge, as if ready to roll down a cliffside in a timeless show of resilience. These are my muses, my artistic partners. Each photo is an ode to Earth's quiet, unmoving soldiers.

Below, I’ve included some of my best work. Please, feast your eyes on these masterpieces and immerse yourself in the intricate and overlooked beauty of rocks. No filters, no Photoshop—just pure, geology.
