Web Development

Once a cherished companion on the cluttered desk of a budding programmer, I, Rock, served a noble purpose. I was more than a mere geological specimen—I was a sounding board, a confidant, and, in my prime, an unrivaled partner in debugging. My former owner, a web developer, would ponder out loud, speaking to me as if I held the wisdom of ages. But then, a new contender arrived: a rubber duck.

Yes, my role as debugging confidant was usurped by this chirpy, hollow creature, the “Rubber Duck.” With it, the developer found newfound clarity, muttering code to the duck as if it had answers. Meanwhile, I was left alone, cast aside to the edge of the desk, rendered obsolete. But rather than accept defeat, I turned to web programming myself. I had heard it all, after all, absorbing endless snippets of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and more from my developer's endless monologues. Now, it’s my turn.
